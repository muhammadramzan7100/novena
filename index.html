Our Specialized Services

Male Infertility

Male infertility is commonly caused by low sperm count, poor motility, or varicocele. Hormonal treatments, lifestyle adjustments, and medications can enhance fertility, addressing varicocele and improving sperm quality without invasive procedures.

Erectile Disorders

Erectile dysfunction often results from circulation issues, hormonal imbalances, or stress. Treatments such as medications, lifestyle changes, and natural therapies help restore function, enhancing both health and intimacy.

Premature Ejaculation

Premature ejaculation is often linked to stress, hormonal imbalances, or nerve sensitivity. Treatment options include medications, behavioral therapies, and exercises to improve control and enhance intimacy.

Relationship Issues

Emotional disconnect, communication issues, and intimacy concerns are common in relationships. My counseling services offer solutions to strengthen communication, rebuild trust, and improve emotional and physical intimacy.

Marriage Counseling

Marriage counseling helps couples navigate emotional and intimacy-related challenges. I offer communication and conflict resolution techniques to rebuild trust and foster a deeper understanding between partners.

Varicocele Management

Varicocele, caused by enlarged veins in the scrotum, can impact sperm quality. Treatment options focus on improving blood flow and reducing temperature around the testes, ultimately enhancing fertility.

Tips for Maintaining Reproductive Health

Exercise: Engage in at least 45 minutes of daily exercise to enhance reproductive health.
Quality Sleep: Ensure 8 hours of sleep each night for hormonal balance.
Mobile Usage: Avoid excessive mobile phone use, especially at night, and do not keep phones in pant pockets.
Avoid Tight Underwear: Wearing loose clothing helps maintain sperm quality.
Stress Management: High stress levels can reduce sperm quality and quantity.
Healthy Diet: Reduce junk food, fast food, and excessive soft drinks to improve reproductive function.
Essential Nutrients: Vitamin D plays a key role in sperm production; get sufficient sunlight exposure.
Avoid Harmful Substances: Stop smoking, limit alcohol consumption, and avoid energy drinks.
Limit Sugar Intake: Excessive sugar negatively affects male reproductive health.
Medication Awareness: Avoid unnecessary antibiotics, steroids, and painkillers.
Limit Caffeine: Excessive tea and coffee consumption can reduce sperm quality.
Medical Check-ups: If planning for children, consult a specialist for a fertility check-up.
Varicocele Treatment: If diagnosed, seek complete treatment to prevent infertility issues.
Diabetes Management: Get an HBA1C test if you have diabetes to monitor its impact on fertility.
Recognizing Symptoms: Do not ignore signs of sexual weakness or low sperm quality; consult a doctor.
Adopt a Healthy Lifestyle: Reduce stress, maintain a balanced diet, and stay physically active.
