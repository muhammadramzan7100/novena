PRP Therapy for Penile Health

A natural, non-surgical solution for Erectile Dysfunction and penile rejuvenation.

At SAMC, we offer advanced PRP (Platelet-Rich Plasma) therapy tailored to male sexual health.

A small sample of your blood is drawn and processed in a centrifuge to concentrate platelets and growth factors.

This treatment is especially effective for men facing mild to moderate Erectile Dysfunction seeking natural alternatives.

Benefits of PRP Therapy:
  • Improved erection strength and duration
  • Enhanced sensitivity and sexual performance
  • Better blood flow in penile tissues
  • Natural growth of penile muscle tissue
  • No foreign substances, no downtime

Testicular Biopsy

A diagnostic and therapeutic procedure for men with azoospermia (zero sperm count).

Testicular biopsy is a minimally invasive procedure performed when semen analysis reveals zero sperm count, a condition known as azoospermia. This procedure helps determine whether sperm production is occurring within the testicles and if any obstruction is preventing sperm from appearing in the ejaculate.

During the biopsy, a small tissue sample is extracted from the testicle under local anesthesia. This sample is then examined microscopically to assess sperm production and identify any underlying issues. In some cases, viable sperm retrieved during the biopsy can be used for assisted reproductive techniques like IVF or ICSI.

Testicular biopsy is crucial for diagnosing the cause of azoospermia and guiding appropriate treatment options, offering hope to couples facing male infertility challenges.

Key Benefits of Testicular Biopsy
  • Accurate diagnosis of azoospermia causes
  • Determines presence of sperm production
  • Guides appropriate fertility treatment options
  • Potential to retrieve sperm for IVF/ICSI
  • Minimally invasive with quick recovery

Microvaricocelectomy (MSV)

A safe, effective surgical solution for varicocele-related male infertility.

Varicocele is a common but often overlooked condition where the veins within the scrotum become enlarged, causing impaired blood flow. This can lead to poor semen quality, pain, and eventually, infertility. Many patients experience discomfort, low sperm count, or abnormal semen reports without knowing the underlying cause.

Microvaricocelectomy (MSV) is a highly advanced, minimally invasive surgery that corrects this issue using a microscope for precision. Unlike medications that provide only short-term improvement in semen quality, MSV offers a permanent solution by addressing the root cause.

Studies and real-world cases show that MSV significantly improves sperm parameters — even in patients with very low or zero sperm count — enabling natural conception. Many couples who had lost hope of conceiving have successfully had children after undergoing this procedure.

If you or your partner is struggling with infertility and has been diagnosed with varicocele, Microvaricocelectomy is a medically proven treatment with high success rates and minimal downtime.

Key Benefits of MSV Surgery
  • Significant improvement in sperm count and quality
  • Permanent solution compared to short-term medication
  • Minimal post-op pain and fast recovery
  • High success rate in natural conception
  • Performed by specialists using microsurgical techniques

Vasoepididymostomy

Advanced microsurgical solution for obstructive azoospermia

Vasoepididymostomy is a precision microsurgical procedure performed to bypass blockages in the male reproductive system, specifically where the vas deferens meets the epididymis. This delicate surgery is recommended for men with obstructive azoospermia where sperm production is normal but blocked from reaching the semen.

Using high-powered surgical microscopes and specialized instruments, our surgeons create a new connection between the vas deferens and the epididymis, allowing sperm to flow naturally again. This procedure requires exceptional surgical skill due to the microscopic size of the structures involved (often less than 0.5mm in diameter).

Considered the gold standard for treating epididymal obstructions, vasoepididymostomy offers eligible patients the chance to achieve natural conception without needing sperm extraction procedures.

Key Advantages of Vasoepididymostomy
  • Restores natural sperm flow in obstructive cases
  • Higher success rates with microsurgical techniques
  • Minimally invasive approach with small incision
  • Preserves reproductive tract integrity
  • Enables natural conception possibilities
